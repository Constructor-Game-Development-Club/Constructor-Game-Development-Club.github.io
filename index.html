Constructor Game Development Club

CGDC is where students at Constructor make video games rather than only play them. We run weekly working sessions, weekend game jams, and public demo nights.

Members work in teams they form themselves, across programming, design, art and writing. Everything made at a CGDC event gets published with your name on it, and you keep full ownership of what you create.

No experience required. Games need artists, writers, composers and playtesters as much as they need programmers.

What we do

Every Thursday, 19:00

Open Sessions

Bring a laptop and a project, or bring nothing and find people to work with. No agenda, no signup, no obligation to make anything.

Roughly monthly, weekends

Game Jams

Three days to take an idea to a finished, playable game. Teams form on the first evening. Every entry is published and the creators keep all rights to their work.

Recorded and published

Lectures

Talks on how games are made, from the disciplines involved to the craft of design. Open to the whole campus and posted to our channel afterwards.

Join us

CGDC is open to every student at Constructor, from any programme and any year. You do not need to know how to code — games need artists, writers, composers, designers and playtesters just as much as programmers.

Come to an Open Session on any Thursday. You do not need to sign up first.
