Until the theme is revealed.

Three days to make a game from scratch. We give you a theme on Friday evening, you build in a small team, and by Sunday night you have something people can play. Any engine, any language.

Sign up here — sign-ups close Wednesday 14 October.

Never done a jam?

Start here. Four things people usually worry about, answered.

I cannot code

You do not need to. Games need art, music, sound, writing, level design and playtesting. Every team needs someone who is not programming, and some of the best jam games are made by people who have never written a line of code.

I have no idea what I am doing

Neither do most people at their first jam. Our lecture on 8 October walks through exactly how to approach a weekend build, and there are people in the room all weekend who will help you.

I do not have a team

Sign up alone. We place you in a team on the Friday, balanced so that every team has the skills it needs. Most people arrive knowing nobody.

My game will be bad

Everyone's first jam game is bad. That is the point — you learn more in one weekend than in a month of watching tutorials. We score heavily on whether a game is finished, not on whether it is impressive. A small game that works beats an ambitious one that does not.

Rules

Teams

Two to four people, three recommended. Teams form on the Friday evening.

What you can bring

Any engine, language or tool. Personal libraries and general-purpose code written before the jam are fine. Licensed or public-domain asset packs are fine if properly credited. Pre-existing game code, or a project started before the theme reveal, is not.

AI

AI code assistance is allowed and must be declared in your itch.io description. AI-generated art, music, sound and writing are not eligible — those must be made by your team or come from properly credited licensed sources. Undeclared use of AI disqualifies an entry.

Submitting

An itch.io page, browser-playable if possible, by 20:00 on Sunday. Your description must list your team, the tools you used, and any AI code assistance.

Your work is yours

Participants keep all rights to everything they create. CGDC only asks for permission to show and archive entries when promoting the club.
