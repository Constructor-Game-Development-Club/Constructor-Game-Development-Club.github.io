Lectures

A series on how games actually get made. Each lecture is open to the whole campus, recorded, and published afterwards along with its slides and sources. No background needed for any of them.

This semester

Thursday 24 September, 19:00

#1 — How are games made?

A walk through how a game gets built: the disciplines involved, what each one actually does day to day, the skills each needs, and how a project moves from concept to prototype to shipped game. Where indie and AAA differ, and where they do not.

Thursday 8 October, 19:00

#2 — Introduction to Game Design

What game design actually is: rules, systems and the loop. The MDA framework worked through on a game everyone knows, a few of Jesse Schell's lenses applied live, and then designing under constraint — why your first idea is too big and how to shrink it into something you can finish in a weekend.
